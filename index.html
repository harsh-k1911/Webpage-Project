ATTRACTIONS

Udaipur - The City of Lakes

Welcome to Udaipur, the enchanting "City of Lakes" of Rajasthan. The city reflects a stunning vision of white marble palaces and timeless elegance. Often called the "Venice of the East".

Visit

Jaipur - The Pink City

Welcome to Jaipur, the vibrant "Pink City" of Rajasthan guarded by the majestic Amer Fort. The city opens up in a stunning panorama of terracotta-pink and historic grandeur.

Visit

Jodhpur - The Blue City

Welcome to Jodhpur, the magnificent "Blue City" of Rajasthan. It is a captivating blend of royal grandeur, rich history, and the timeless spirit of the Marwar kingdom.

Visit

Bikaner - The Red City

Bikaner is situated in the northern region of Rajasthan. Bikaner still displays its ancient opulence through palaces and forts, built of red sandstone. The city boasts of some of the world's best riding camels

Visit

Jaisalmer - The Golden City

Welcome to Jaisalmer, the magnificent "Golden City" of Rajasthan. Rising like a mirage from the heart of the Thar Desert. aisalmer is a captivating blend of intricate haveli architecture, rich caravan history.

Visit

Kumbalgarh

Welcome to Kumbhalgarh, the formidable "Great Wall of India" in Rajasthan. As you walk along its immense, you'll discover that this imposing wall is not just a structure, but a powerful symbol of Rajput resilience.
